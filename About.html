About Paarambariya Virundhu

Where Every Meal Feels Like Home

Paarambariya Virunthu is inspired by the timeless Tamil tradition of welcoming guests with warmth, generosity, and delicious food.

Our restaurant brings together traditional South Indian flavours and a warm dining experience. Every dish is prepared with carefully selected ingredients and recipes inspired by generations of Tamil cooking.

From a comforting plate of traditional meals to aromatic biryani and freshly prepared desserts, we believe that every meal should create a memorable experience.
